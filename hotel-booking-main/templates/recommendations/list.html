How the score works

Each feature is normalized from 0 to 1, multiplied by its configured weight, and summed to a percentage. Rooms are sorted by score, then lower nightly price, then database ID for deterministic ties. Budget is a soft preference: full credit at or below budget, tapering to zero at 150% of budget.
