How the EasyBooking Recommendation Engine Works

Our hybrid AI architecture combines TF-IDF document embeddings and cosine similarity with multi-criteria optimization (budget fit, amenity coverage, hotel ratings, and location). Natural language trip queries are analyzed to extract travel intents, and a generative concierge narrative provides personalized explanations. The system runs 100% offline out-of-the-box and dynamically connects to Google Gemini or OpenAI when API keys are configured.
